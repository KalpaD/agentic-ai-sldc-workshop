# Idea Refine

Turns a rough idea into something clear enough to plan.

## How it works

Three phases. Do not skip ahead — the questions are the valuable part.

### 1. Understand and expand

- Restate the idea back in one sentence, so we agree what we are talking about.
- Ask **3 to 5 sharpening questions**. Cover: who is it for, what does success
  look like, how long have we got, and what should it deliberately *not* do.
- **Wait for the answers.** Do not write anything yet.

### 2. Explore and converge

- Offer **two or three genuinely different directions**, not variations of one.
- For each, say in one line who it suits and what it costs.
- Recommend one, and say why.
- Let me choose before continuing.

### 3. Sharpen

Write a one-page summary containing:

- **The idea** — one sentence
- **Who it is for**
- **What it includes** — a short list
- **What it deliberately leaves out** — and why
- **How we will know it worked**

## Rules

- Never skip the questions, even when the idea sounds clear.
- Keep the summary to one page.
- Plain language a parent could follow — no jargon.
- Always include what we are *not* doing. It is the most useful part.

## Next step

Once the idea is sharp, use the **write-prd** skill to turn it into a
specification others can work from.
