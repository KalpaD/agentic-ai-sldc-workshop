# Course Outline

## Topic

$ARGUMENTS

## Produce an outline with these four parts

1. **Who it is for** — one line naming the year group and what they already know.
2. **Learning outcomes** — 3 to 5 things a student can *do* by the end.
3. **Sessions** — 4 to 6 sessions, each with a title and one line on what happens in it.
4. **Check they learned it** — one simple way to assess, no formal exam.

## Rules

- Keep the whole outline to one page.
- Use plain language a parent could follow — no jargon.
- Give each session one practical activity rather than a long explanation.
- If the topic or year group is unclear, ask me before writing.
