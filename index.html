How to use these materials

  • Slides open in any browser. Use ← and → to move, f for fullscreen, n for speaker notes.
  • Student guides repeat everything said in the room, plus a command reference and a "when something goes wrong" table. Every one of them prints cleanly if you'd rather have paper.
  • The .md files are ordinary text. Open one, select all, and paste it to your agent — or ask your agent to write it for you, which is what we do in the sessions.
  • Nothing here expires. The tools are free and the files are yours. Everything keeps working on Monday morning, and on your students' machines too.
